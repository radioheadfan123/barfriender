Privacy

Last updated October 3, 2026

Without an account

You can use Mix without signing in. What you're working on (your current mix, the "bar only" setting) is kept in your own browser's storage so it's still there when you come back. It never leaves your device. If you sign in, your sign-in session is kept there too.

With an account

We store your email address and a securely hashed password (handled by Supabase, our database and sign-in provider), the drinks and combos you save, and the menus you create or join.

  • Your saved drinks and combos are private to you.
  • In a shared menu, the other members can see the menu's drinks and your email address in the member list.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we don't sell or share your data.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the database and handles sign-in.
  • Railway hosts the website.
  • Resend delivers feedback messages to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

Error reports

If something breaks in your browser, the page sends us the error message, the file and line, and which page you were on, so we can fix it. It doesn't include your email or anything you've saved.

Feedback

If you send feedback or a bug report, we receive your message, the email you typed (if any), the page you came from and your browser type. It reaches us by email through Resend, and we use it only to reply and to improve the site.

Deleting your data

Open your account (your email in the top corner) and choose Delete my account. That permanently deletes your account and your private saves. Drinks you added to shared menus stay for the other members, without your name on them, and menus you own pass to another member.
